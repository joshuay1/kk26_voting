Voter Representation by Winning Outcome Number of funded projects each voter approved (Ja or EherJa) — n=24 voters
Shows how many winning projects each voter actively liked ("Ja" or "Eher Ja"). A fairer outcome means more people get roughly the same number of projects. A flatter curve means voters are winning a very wide range of projects, leaving some people with almost nothing and others with a lot.
Gini Coefficient (Inequality)
Rates the inequality of the chart to the left. A score of 0.0 is perfect equality; higher numbers are worse. Local MES delivers the fairest outcome for all voters.
